UzCheckers: Maxfiylik siyosati

UzCheckers (uzcheckers.netlify.app) onlayn shashka saytidir.

Qanday ma'lumot yig'amiz

Google, Facebook yoki Telegram orqali kirsangiz: ismingiz, profil rasmingiz, (Google va Facebook uchun) elektron pochtangiz, (Telegram uchun) Telegram ID va foydalanuvchi nomingiz. Mehmon sifatida kirsangiz, faqat tasodifiy hisob identifikatori yaratiladi. Shuningdek o'yinlaringiz tarixi va sozlamalaringiz (doska ranglari, til) saqlanadi.

Nima uchun ishlatamiz

Hisobingizni yuritish, o'yinlar tarixi va sozlamalarni saqlash, do'stlar bilan o'ynash imkonini berish uchun. Ma'lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz va reklama uchun ishlatmaymiz.

Kimlar bilan bo'lishamiz

Ma'lumotlar xizmat ko'rsatuvchilar orqali saqlanadi va qayta ishlanadi: Supabase (kirish va ma'lumotlar bazasi), Netlify (sayt hosting), shuningdek siz tanlagan kirish provayderi (Google, Facebook, Telegram). Brauzerda sozlamalar va kirish sessiyasi uchun localStorage ishlatiladi.

Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Hisobingiz va u bilan bog'liq barcha ma'lumotlarni o'chirish uchun ELEKTRON-POCHTA-MANZILINGIZ manziliga "Hisobni o'chirish" mavzusi bilan xat yozing. So'rovni iloji boricha tezroq bajaramiz.

UzCheckers: Privacy Policy

UzCheckers (uzcheckers.netlify.app) is an online checkers website.

Data we collect. If you sign in with Google, Facebook or Telegram: your name, profile picture, (Google and Facebook) email address, and (Telegram) Telegram ID and username. Guests only get a random account identifier. We also store your game history and settings.

How we use it. To run your account, keep your game history and settings, and let you play with friends. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it. Supabase (authentication and database), Netlify (hosting) and the sign-in provider you choose. Your browser's localStorage is used for settings and your login session.

Data deletion. To delete your account and all related data, email ELEKTRON-POCHTA-MANZILINGIZ with the subject "Delete my account". We will handle the request as soon as possible.
